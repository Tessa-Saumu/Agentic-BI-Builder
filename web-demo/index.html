Online mode uses Google Gemini. Get a key from Google AI Studio ↗.

The key stays in this page’s memory and goes directly to Google. It is never saved and is cleared after each run. Google’s rate limits and billing apply to your account.

What is sent to Google?

The data profile: column names, types, row count, missing values, and distinct counts. No complete CSV or row samples are sent. Column names and counts can still be sensitive; use data you are comfortable sharing.

Take it into Power BI
The ZIP contains a .pbip project, report definition, semantic model, and your CSV. A .pbip is an editable Power BI project; a .pbix is the single-file report saved by Desktop.
  1. Right-click the downloaded ZIP and choose Extract All. Do not open the .pbip from inside the ZIP: it needs the other folders beside it.
  2. Inside the extracted folder, run prepare_report.ps1 in PowerShell to point the report at the included CSV. Or follow OPEN_IN_POWER_BI.txt for the manual option.
  3. Open the extracted BrowserDemo.pbip in Power BI Desktop and refresh the data.
Power BI Desktop is required on Windows. This browser demo cannot open Desktop or verify its rendering on your computer.
